About Us

Dressing women with grace

MAÉLORA began as a small boutique with one belief: every woman deserves clothes that make her feel graceful, whether she is dressing for a wedding or a Sunday market.

We pair traditional craft with modern cuts, and we choose soft fabrics, honest fits and finishing you can feel. Each piece is checked by hand before it leaves our studio.
